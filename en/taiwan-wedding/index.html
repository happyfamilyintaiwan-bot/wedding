FAQ

Questions

What is in the Taiwan wedding package, and how much is it?

A couple portrait, a marriage certificate design (PDF), a bilingual Chinese and English invitation website, a guide for overseas guests, a bilingual RSVP form template, a welcome sign file and a save-the-date image. The package is US$690 (or NT$22,000).

How do we pay from abroad?

Pay in US dollars by Wise, or in Taiwan dollars by bank transfer to a Taiwan account. We send payment details after confirming your order, and start after full payment.

Can we get a refund?

Yes, a full refund if you cancel before we start the first sketch. Once drawing has started, we can't offer a refund.

We're not in Taiwan. How do we receive everything?

Everything in the package is digital and delivered by email. If you add printing, we send it by registered mail to your address in Taiwan.

Do you see our guests' RSVP replies?

No. You create the RSVP form in your own Google account, so replies are stored only in your account.

Can the invitation include other languages?

Yes, Japanese or Spanish can be added. Tell us when you get in touch.

Can we use the certificate for marriage registration in Taiwan?

It is designed in the marriage certificate format used for household registration in Taiwan. For the documents and steps you need, please follow the rules of the Household Registration Office.

How long does it take?

Usually 4 to 5 weeks. Please contact us at least 2 months before your wedding. Less than 4 weeks is a rush order: an extra 30% of the total, and only if our schedule allows.

Do you work with same-sex couples?

Yes. The certificate uses the matching legal wording.
